1. Statement of Compliance

Eagler Versions respects the intellectual property rights of creators and copyright holders. It is our policy to respond expeditiously to legitimate notices of alleged copyright infringement in accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) (Title 17, United States Code, Section 512).

2. Open-Source Software & Clean-Room Transpilation

The web clients hosted on this portal are compiled using open-source tools including TeaVM, WebAssembly bytecode compilation, and OpenGL-to-WebGL emulation layers developed by community contributors (such as LAX1DUDE and community reverse engineers).

3. Submitting a DMCA Notice

If you are a copyright owner or authorized representative and believe that any content hosted on this mirror infringes upon your copyrights, please submit a formal written notification with the following details:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of the person authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  3. Identification of the material on our website that you claim is infringing, with exact URLs.
  4. Sufficient contact information (your name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address).
  5. A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement made under penalty of perjury that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.
