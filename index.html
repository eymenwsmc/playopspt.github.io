How Eaglercraft Works

Key details for saving worlds, joining servers, and optimizing performance.

Singleplayer Worlds

Your worlds are stored in IndexedDB. You can export world backup files (.epk) from the Singleplayer menu anytime to keep them safe or move them to another computer.

Multiplayer Servers

Multiplayer connects using WebSocket addresses (starting with wss://). Any server running EaglercraftXServer (on Spigot, BungeeCord, or Velocity) can be joined directly from the Multiplayer menu.

Custom Skins

You can upload custom player skins in the game options. Both classic 64x32 and modern 64x64 skin formats are supported.

Performance Tips

If you experience frame drops on older hardware or Chromebooks, set render distance to 4–6 chunks and turn off smooth lighting in Video Settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a Minecraft account?

No. You can pick a custom username and jump right into singleplayer or community servers.


Will clearing my browser history delete my worlds?

Yes. Browser data wipes include IndexedDB storage. Make regular world backups using the Export World button in the singleplayer screen.
